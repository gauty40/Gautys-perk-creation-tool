CUSTOM PERKS
============

19 new perks for Zombies, each with its own major and minor augments, plus a custom perk menu,
HUD icons with charge bars, eternal perk frames and custom perk machines.

INSTALL
1. Drag the "custom_perks" folder into your  kronos\mods  folder
   (you should end up with  kronos\mods\custom_perks\mod.json).
2. Open the Kronos launcher and enable "Custom Perks".
3. Fully restart the game.

In co-op, every player needs the mod installed.

USING IT
- Buy the perks from the custom perk machines or Der Wunderfizz.
- Pick augments in the Augments menu, under each perk.
- Turn individual perks on or off in the CUSTOM PERKS menu on the Zombies main menu.

FOR OTHER MOD MAKERS
The augment sync uses the perk_choice_purchase channel with values 4000-20999 and 22000-22999.
23000 on the same channel closes the slot machine interface (Esc).
Please don't reuse those ranges (Ring of Fire uses 21000-21999).

UNINSTALL
Disable "Custom Perks" in the launcher, or delete the custom_perks folder, and restart.
